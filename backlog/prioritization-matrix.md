# Prioritization Matrix
Adjusted Priority = RICE Score + Risk Modifier + Compliance Modifier + Adoption Modifier + Executive Urgency Modifier

RICE alone can undervalue low-reach but high-risk or audit-sensitive work. Modifiers ensure governance and adoption needs are visible.

Priority labels:
- P0: Immediate focus
- P1: Current cycle commit candidate
- P2: Next cycle candidate
- P3: Defer or route to operations
