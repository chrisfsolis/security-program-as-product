# Adoption Operating Cadence

## Why Adoption Matters
Security programs create value when teams consistently use new workflows and controls—not when capabilities are merely released.

## Delivery vs. Adoption
Delivery confirms that a feature shipped. Adoption confirms that behavior changed and outcomes improved (for example: fewer repeat issues, better control evidence, faster decisions).

## Weekly Execution Review
Focus:
- New releases and immediate usage signals
- Open blockers and remediation progress
- Intake queue changes and capacity constraints

Outputs:
- Updated action list with owners and due dates
- Risks escalated for monthly review if unresolved

## Monthly Risk/Control Review
Focus:
- Control ownership coverage
- Evidence collection status and exceptions
- Trend in unresolved risk items

Outputs:
- Exception decisions and mitigation plans
- Updated risk register priorities

## Quarterly Roadmap Refresh
Focus:
- Re-score major initiatives
- Adjust sequencing based on adoption, risk, and capacity
- Confirm executive priorities for next quarter

Outputs:
- Revised roadmap milestones
- Communicated scope changes and rationale

## Adoption Metrics
- % teams using new workflow
- SLA adherence after rollout
- Reduction in repeat support/security issues
- Control owners assigned
- Evidence collected on schedule
- Executive decisions logged
- Remediation work closed within SLA

## Owner Model
- **Program Manager / TPM:** cadence facilitation, roadmap updates, escalation tracking
- **Security Operations Lead:** workflow adoption and issue trend monitoring
- **Control Owners / GRC Partners:** evidence readiness and exception management
- **Security Leadership:** tradeoff decisions and priority approvals

## Example Adoption Signals
- Team adoption rises from pilot to multi-team use within one quarter
- SLA adherence improves after rollout stabilization period
- Repeat issue category volume declines month over month
- Control evidence submission timeliness improves by review cycle
- Executive decision log shows faster closure of priority decisions
