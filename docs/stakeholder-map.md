# Stakeholder Map

## Stakeholder Groups

| Stakeholder | Role in Program | Primary Interests | Influence | Engagement Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CISO | Executive sponsor | Risk reduction, investment ROI, regulatory confidence | High | Monthly + quarterly |
| CIO/CTO | Strategic partner | Platform reliability, engineering trade-offs, delivery velocity | High | Monthly |
| Security TPM/Program Manager | Program owner | Roadmap execution, dependency management, reporting quality | High | Weekly |
| SecOps Manager | Operational owner | Alert quality, incident handling, analyst capacity | High | Weekly |
| Security Engineering Lead | Technical delivery lead | Integrations, automation, scalability | High | Weekly |
| GRC Lead | Assurance owner | Control design/effectiveness, audit readiness | Medium-High | Biweekly |
| Cloud Platform Lead | Dependency owner | Infrastructure ownership, remediation feasibility | Medium-High | Weekly |
| Engineering Managers | Remediation partners | Backlog impact, SLA compliance, prioritization fairness | Medium | Biweekly |
| Internal Audit | Assurance stakeholder | Evidence quality, repeatable controls | Medium | Monthly |
| Finance/PMO | Planning stakeholder | Budget adherence, milestone confidence | Medium | Monthly/quarterly |

## RACI by Workstream

| Workstream | Accountable | Responsible | Consulted | Informed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cloud security monitoring | SecOps Manager | SecOps Analysts, Security Engineering | Cloud Platform Lead | CISO, CIO |
| Vulnerability management | Security TPM | Security Engineering, Engineering Managers | Asset Owners, GRC | CISO, CIO |
| Control validation / GRC automation | GRC Lead | GRC Analysts, Security Engineering | Internal Audit | CISO, CTO |
| Incident response readiness | SecOps Manager | Incident Commander, IR team | Legal, Communications, IT Ops | CISO, CIO |
| Security metrics & executive reporting | Security TPM | TPM + BI Analyst | CISO Staff, GRC, SecOps | Executive leadership |
