# Program Charter

## Program Purpose
Establish and run a cybersecurity program using a product-style operating model that improves risk visibility, delivery consistency, and executive decision quality.

## Scope
In scope:
- Stakeholder request intake and triage
- Roadmap planning and release communication
- Backlog management and prioritization
- Risk and control tracking
- Adoption and outcome measurement

## Target Stakeholders
- Security leadership (strategy, risk posture, funding priorities)
- Engineering and platform teams (execution, remediation, workflow alignment)
- Risk, compliance, and audit partners (control evidence, traceability)
- Executive leadership (tradeoffs, decisions, operating risk)

## Business Outcomes
- Faster, clearer decisions on security investments
- Reduced operational risk through prioritized execution
- Improved control ownership and audit readiness
- Better adoption of high-value workflows across teams

## Guiding Principles
- Prioritize outcomes over activity volume
- Make tradeoffs explicit and decision-ready
- Balance risk reduction with delivery capacity
- Measure adoption and behavior change, not just release output
- Keep artifacts practical, concise, and reviewable

## Governance Cadence
- Weekly: execution review (delivery status, blockers, intake updates)
- Monthly: risk/control review (exceptions, evidence, ownership)
- Quarterly: roadmap refresh (re-prioritization, sequencing, investment decisions)

## Success Measures
- Increased percent of teams adopting target workflows
- Improved SLA adherence post-rollout
- Reduced repeat security/support issues
- Higher on-time control evidence collection
- Faster closure of remediation work within agreed SLA

## Out of Scope
- Building production security tooling
- Storing sensitive customer or proprietary company data
- Replacing formal GRC systems or SIEM platforms
