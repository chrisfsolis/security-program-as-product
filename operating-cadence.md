# Operating Cadence
## Weekly intake review
Inputs: new requests, stakeholder context, existing backlog. Outputs: status updates, discovery tasks, routing decisions.
## Weekly execution review
Inputs: prioritized backlog, blockers, delivery progress. Outputs: sprint commitments, dependency escalations, recommendation changes.
## Monthly risk/control review
Inputs: risk log, control evidence status, remediation actions. Outputs: mitigations, accepted risks, overdue escalations.
## Monthly adoption review
Inputs: adoption metrics, friction notes, shipped capability list. Outputs: interventions, enablement plans, ownership actions.
## Quarterly roadmap refresh
Inputs: phase outcomes, capacity constraints, leadership priorities. Outputs: updated phase deliverables and exit criteria.
## Executive decision review
Inputs: decision-needed items with impact and tradeoffs. Outputs: explicit decisions, owners, and due dates logged.
